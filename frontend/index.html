Router Placement & Channel Optimization Guide

Open Router Gateway
1. Separate 2.4 GHz & 5/6 GHz

Many routers combine bands under one SSID. Split them or force your PC to connect to the 5 GHz / 6 GHz network for 5x to 10x higher throughput and zero microwave interference.

2. Fixed Clean Channel

Disable "Auto Channel" on your router. Set 2.4 GHz strictly to Channel 1, 6, or 11. On 5 GHz, use Channel 36, 44, or 149 (based on our Spectrum tab analysis).

3. Elevate & Clear Path

Keep the router at least 3-4 feet above floor level away from metal cabinets, mirrors, aquariums, and power bricks to eliminate multipath reflection and packet loss.
